What is TGDValues?

TGDValues is a joke political test for The Geography Discord. Don't take the questions or the results seriously at all. Based on the original 8values concept.

There are questions in the test.

What are the eight values?

There are four independent axes - Focus, Diplomatic, Civil, and Generation - and each has two opposing values assigned to them. They are:

MAINPLOT

Those with higher Mainplot scores focus mostly on the main discord, and not on outside ventures like Countryvia or Geography.gg

FOCUS

VENTURES

Those with higher Ventures scores support work on outside ventures like Countryvia and Geography.gg

TGDIST

Those with higher TGDist scores believe in TGD supperiority, it's the best (geography) discord

DIPLOMATIC

GLOBALIST

Those with higher Globalist scores are losers who also chat in other discord servers

LIBERTY

Those with higher Liberty scores are dangerous anarchists who deny the authority of the regime and don't listen to moderation

CIVIL

MODERATION

Those with higher Moderation scores agree that the regime knows best and obey the moderators

OLDGEN

Those with higher Oldgen scores have that because they feel superior for having joined a few months before others have

GENERATION

NEWGEN

Those with higher Newgen scores are usually new to the server


What's the "Closest Match" mean at the bottom of the results?

In addition to matching you to the eight values, the quiz also attempts to match you to a political ideology. This is a work in progress and is much less accurate than the values and axes, so don't take it too seriously. If you disagree with your assigned ideology, send me (HippoEXE) a discord message!
